Contact by email and WhatsApp

The request forms on this website do not send any data themselves. They only prepare your message in your email app or in WhatsApp, and you decide there whether to send it. When you write to me, I process your details (e.g. name, contact details, message and, for a reading, your birth details) to answer your request and prepare a booking. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual steps) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering general enquiries). If you voluntarily share health information with me, for example before a Kambo ceremony, I process it only with your consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) and only to accompany you safely. I delete your data as soon as it is no longer needed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Links to social networks and Etsy

This website links to my profiles on Instagram, YouTube, SoundCloud and to my Etsy shop. These are plain links. No data is sent to these providers unless you click them. After clicking, the respective provider's privacy policy applies.
