What is Kambo?

Kambo (also known as “frog medicine”) is obtained from the secretion of the Amazon frog Phyllomedusa bicolor.

For hundreds of years, various Amazonian tribes have used this traditional medicine - according to their tradition, to strengthen their hunting skills, to cleanse body, mind and soul, and to remove negative energy, the so-called “panema”.

Today many people outside the Amazon come to Kambo as well, for example with psychological complaints, chronic pain or addiction, or at a time when they want to realign themselves physically, emotionally or spiritually.

In its culture of origin, Kambo is regarded as one of the most powerful natural resources for body, mind and soul.

The secretion contains a variety of natural peptides, such as dermorphin, that are also studied by science. How Kambo affects you is very personal.

What people share after a ceremony

  • a feeling of deep cleansing
  • more clarity and awakened senses
  • new strength and aliveness
  • emotional & spiritual blockages are allowed to release
  • space to let go of painful experiences in the mental and emotional body

Kambo does not replace medical or psychotherapeutic treatment. Before we arrange a ceremony, we take time to talk about your health.
