Music

Live music, DJ sets and hybrid sets.

Booking requestSoundCloud

Whatever I do - singing is part of it. My mother says I sang before I could speak.

My music accompanies me on this endlessly magical, colourful journey we call life, wherever I set my bare feet on Mother Earth. Whether at gatherings, in my sacred plant medicine ceremonies and retreats, at my solo concerts or during my DJ sets: I sing.

I have collaborated with amazing musicians like Natan Rabin, Mose, Goyanu and many more, and recently opened the show of YAIMA with my concert.

My musical offering ranges from medicine songs, icaros and world & folk music to new interpretations of old classic songs that touch people’s hearts and souls.

My concerts always have a deeply ceremonial character. They fuse my own spiritual journey with my healing work and so become a prayer offered to Life and to the journey we are all embarking on - as One Tribe of the New Earth.
