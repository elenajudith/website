I have been offering Yoga Nidra for almost 20 years: in courses, workshops and retreats, at festivals such as Ozora and MoDem, in one-to-one sessions and online.

Yoga Nidra is a guided deep meditation that has its origins in the ancient Indian tradition of Tantra.

Through deep relaxation of body, mind and soul and the shift of our brain waves to theta waves (the same waves that are activated in hypnosis), we enter a trance-like space between waking and sleep.

In this way we gain direct access to our subconscious, to everything unconscious within us and to the astral dimensions of consciousness.

Direct access to your subconscious

Because we work directly with the subconscious, everything we tell ourselves in this state, and everything we hear and experience, has an immense, deep and valuable influence.

An impulse planted in the subconscious can unfold in our lives far more easily than one given in the waking state!

We also learn to distinguish more clearly between states such as waking, sleeping, dozing and dreaming and to become more lucid, that is, more aware and awake, which also helps us to have more pleasant dreams and nights. In this way we practise lucid dreaming and out-of-body experiences (astral travel).

Hypnotherapy meets lucid dreaming meets epigenetics meets manifestation that works

Yoga Nidra is a holistic method of therapy and healing that has been researched scientifically and is used successfully around the world for sleep disorders, anxiety, nightmares, in trauma therapy and much more.

For years I have been able to observe in the participants of my Yoga Nidra courses how this journey into the subconscious not only heals deep-seated traumas and wounds, but also stimulates writing and creative processes in a magical and very deep way and lets them unfold.
